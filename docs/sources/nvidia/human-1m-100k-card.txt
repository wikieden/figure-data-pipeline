Human-1M is the largest and most diverse high-quality egocentric dataset which contains egocentric video, dense language annotations, head and hand motion focused on real world manipulation. Human-1M spans economically valuable work in the wild and in controlled studios, and powers NVIDIA's flagship Cosmos and GR00T models.

Ready for commercial and non-commercial use.

## Anatomy of an episode

Video, motion, and language come together at the episode level. **T1 captures the overall goal; T2 breaks the task into timed actions.** Stereo and wrist views connect the scene to head and hand motion.

**Bundle flower stems** connects synchronized stereo and wrist video, six timed T2 actions, stereo 3D reconstruction, head and hand tracks, and robot motion retargeting in one complete demonstration.

[![Bundle flower stems: synchronized stereo and wrist views, a matching 3D reconstruction with head and hand tracks, the T1 goal, and timed T2 actions.](figures/card_episode_anatomy.png)](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Human-1M-Explorer)

**The episode is Human-1M’s atomic unit:** a proficient demonstrator performs economically useful physical work toward a discrete, measurable goal, usually achievable within 5–10 minutes. Video requirements are at least **30 FPS / 480p**, unaltered speed, visible and in-focus hands and task-relevant objects, and one or more hands in frame for **at least 95% of the episode**.

**T1** is a 1–2 sentence summary of the primary goal, major actions, and key objects. **T2** specifies a contiguous sequence of **1–10-second segments** covering the episode, bounded by observable action intent. Each segment specifies hand laterality, precise verbs, and unambiguous objects, and is interpretable on its own.

Motion annotations include 6-DoF head-camera pose and 21 keypoints per hand, with tracking masks and confidence. [Full episode requirements](QUALITY_STANDARDS.md).

[Explore video, 3D reconstruction, retargeting, and T2 annotations in Spaces →](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Human-1M-Explorer)

## Human-1M diversity

The slice spans **406,815 recorded task clusters, 81,557 scenes, 41,288 collectors, and nine environment categories**. In-the-wild collection captures variation in real homes and workplaces; studios make targeted skills repeatable, including curated tasks, assembly, and in-lab protocols.

From preparing food and stocking shelves to packing orders, assembling parts, and repairing equipment, these demonstrations capture economically valuable human activity. The Space’s **Everyday index of real human work** associates tasks with broad categories from the [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/).

[Explore task clusters, search, and the human-work index](https://huggingface.co/spaces/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Human-1M-Explorer)

## Quality at every step

Four quality gates combine human review and quantitative checks: **high quality dense annotation** with accurate language and timing; **high quality tasks** with useful, clearly visible interactions; **high quality diversity** at the task, scene and collector, and environment levels; and **high quality 3D tracking** checked for alignment, bone-length consistency, and motion smoothness.

## Metadata and dataset structure

The release is organized by **vendor → batch → version**. Each `v0/shard_*/` directory contains a **LeRobot v3.0** dataset. Video, language, and motion align through frame timestamps and episode metadata.

```text
release-staging/oss_100k_v1/
└── vendors/
    └── {vendor_id}/
        └── {batch_id}/
            ├── versions.json
            └── v0/
                └── shard_00/
                    ├── data/
                    │   └── chunk-000/file-000.parquet
                    ├── meta/
                    │   ├── info.json
                    │   ├── episodes/chunk-000/file-000.parquet
                    │   ├── tasks.parquet
                    │   ├── subtasks.parquet
                    │   ├── environments.parquet
                    │   └── scenes.parquet
                    └── videos/
                        └── observation.images.{camera}/
                            └── chunk-000/file-000.mp4
```

| Component | Contents |
|---|---|
| Frame data | Timestamps, head-camera and hand pose, masks, confidence, collector/scene IDs, and label indices. |
| Episode metadata | Episode and rig IDs, anonymous `vendor_id`, camera intrinsics/extrinsics, and data/video intervals. |
| Language and context | Task descriptions, timed subtask occurrences, environment identifiers and labels, and scene descriptions. |
| Video | Monocular or stereo head views, with optional wrist cameras. |
